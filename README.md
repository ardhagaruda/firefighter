# 🚒 Firefighter — HTML Interactive Narrative Game Demo

An immersive, choice-driven interactive narrative game built with web technologies. Step into the boots of a firefighter and navigate high-stakes emergency situations where every decision impacts your team, civilian lives, and the outcome of the rescue operation.

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Firefighter** is a lightweight, web-native narrative adventure demo. Designed as a text-and-choice interactive story, players face real-time decision points under pressure: managing resources, choosing tactical entry routes, evaluating hazardous structures, and executing life-saving rescues.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔀 **Branching Narrative Paths**: Every choice matters. Multiple tactical decisions lead to varied story branches and distinct endings.
- ⏱️ **Real-Time Decision Making**: Experience time-sensitive situations that test critical thinking under pressure.
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast**: Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript — zero external dependencies or heavy engines required.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive UI**: Optimised for seamless gameplay across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
- 📊 **Dynamic Stat Tracking**: Monitor vital parameters such as oxygen levels, team condition, time elapsed, and civilian safety status. (soon)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Read the Scenario**: Analyze the situation brief, ambient conditions, and hazard warnings presented on screen.
2. **Make Your Choice**: Select from available tactical options based on your assessment of risk vs. reward.
3. **Manage Resources**: Keep an eye on your equipment, oxygen supply, and team stamina.
4. **Discover Endings**: Reach different mission outcomes ranging from **Flawless Rescue** to **Tactical Retreat** or **Mission Failure**.
